# Design pillars

Owner's direction (2026-10-01). These are guidance, not hard rules; use them to break ties.

1. **Fun to drive, crush and watch** comes first. Visual spectacle may cheat: effects, camera and lighting don't have to follow the physics.
2. **Realism goes up to that bar and no further.**
   - A car that dies immediately when driving fast is lame.
   - A car that is wrecked and hobbles is funny for a moment but no fun to drive for long.
   - Damage should read clearly and change how the car drives, without making it a chore.
3. **Arcade controls (Burnout), not sim (Assetto Corsa).** The car responds instantly, forgives mistakes, has boost, slides you can hold, and recovers quickly.
4. **Vehicle classes drive like you'd expect**: a steel muscle car, a basic sedan, a truck, and maybe a monster truck or a tank. Stats stay close enough to be competitive in race mode.
5. **One tuning axis, arcade ↔ realistic**, adjusts handling assists and damage severity without rewriting the physics.
