# Cinematic FX

Visual and camera effects that read sim state only. Nothing here feeds back into the physics, so the
physics tests are unchanged. The only sim-side effect is the hit-stop, which scales the sim clock
(`timeWarp`) for 0.09 s on the driven car's big hits, the same way slow-mo already does.

**Tiers.** Choose them with HUD Playback → FX `off / minimal / low / high / Auto`, the `F` key (cycles), or `?fx=off|minimal|low|high` in the URL (for bench A/B). The default is Auto (Defaults restores it); the HUD shows the current tier and whether Auto is on.

**Auto** (`present/auto-fx.ts` `AutoFx`, applied per frame by `fxFrame`). One policy for the whole session, boot to race to reel:
- Load check: boot starts on `minimal`. On a fine-pointer device with a known hardware GPU (`hardwareDesktop`: not SwiftShader, llvmpipe, softpipe or the Basic Render Driver), once `ready` resolves it skips 1.5 s, then counts frames over 1 s. At 57 fps or more (60 less 5 % timer noise, so the post chain has headroom) it switches to `high`. Nothing waits on it.
- Monitor: after every switch it skips 1.5 s (the first post-chain frames), then counts frames over 1 s windows. Two windows in a row under 50 fps (the owner's 60 less 10, judged against 60 whatever the display's refresh) step down one tier: `high` → `low` → `minimal`, and the check starts again on the new tier. A single hitch, however long, lands in one window, so it never drops a tier. Averaging frames per window, not a median interval, catches a 45 fps vsync cadence (16.7, 16.7, 33.3 ms) whose median frame is on time. It never goes below `minimal` and never climbs back during the session: the tier it dropped from failed once already.
- Matches: a race (or a derby) starts on `minimal`, for headroom at the start: from the grid, through the countdown, until green + 3 s (the race clock, or the derby clock that runs from −4.5 s to green at 0). Then the load check runs once more: a 1 s window at 57 fps or more lifts the tier back to the ceiling (the highest tier that has held this session; `low` on a box that dropped to `low`); below that it stays `minimal` until the race ends. From the lift on the monitor runs as usual (two windows under 50 fps drop a tier). When the race ends (results, the reel, back to setup) the tier returns to the ceiling, so a capable desktop watches the results reel on `high`.
- A manual pick (a tier button, `F`, or `?fx=`) turns Auto off for the session; the Auto button or Defaults turns it back on. Each switch logs `Crush Stream FX auto: <tier> (last window <n> fps)` to the console.
- `off`: the renderer draws straight to the canvas. There's no post chain, no mark map, no crash cam, no hit punch, and no tyre smoke from slip. Sparks and glass use the plain look.
- `minimal`: the same straight-to-canvas render as `off` (no HDR target, bloom, grade, vignette, grain or blur; flash, chromatic punch and letterbox need the composite, so they don't show), plus every effect that needs no post: tyre marks (1024² map), tyre smoke and slip sparks, shake and FOV kick, hit-stop, crash-cam cuts. Sparks and glass use the plain look (no streaks, no HDR glow). A fixed-seed capture of `minimal` is identical to `off` (max channel difference 0).
- Every tier's programs and both outputs (canvas, HDR target) are linked and drawn once at boot and when a race course loads, so a tier switch or a race never links a program (`docs/PERF_HITCH.md`).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, flash, chromatic punch, radial blur or crash-cam moves at any tier. The crash-cam cuts still happen but hold still.

Keys: `F` FX tier · `H` night · `X` wet asphalt (also HUD Playback → Night / Wet).

## Thrown drivers (ragdolls)
A driver flies out of a car on a disabling hit: through the windshield on a head-on, or through the struck side's front window on a side hit. Nothing is thrown on a rear hit, a scrape, or a hit that leaves the car sound. The dummy is cosmetic and local: no dummy state enters the sim or the snapshots.
- **Trigger** (`ragdoll-trigger.ts` `EjectionWatch`), once per car per run, on the edge where the car is disabled: its drivetrain dies (a derby kill or wreck, a race DNF by damage), or its engine block is packed back past the kill travel of the slider's realistic end (`killTravel(class, 1, ctx)`: the sourced 0.15 m × durability, × the derby scale in a derby). The second edge is a hit that would disable a real car: at the HUD's default realism (0.25) a fleet sedan only dies at 0.45 m of travel and one head-on tops out at 0.36 m, so no single sandbox or race hit ever kills (by design; the sim is untouched). The hit must be head-on or from a side (`impactInward`), and the peak closing speed over the 0.35 s before it must be at least 6 m/s. Everything it reads (drivetrain flag, `engineTravel`, impact, poses, velocities) is replicated, so a netplay client decides the same way as the host.
- **Rate at default settings** (lane ragdoll-4's headless sweep): fleet 2 cars at 0–32 m/s, 8 of 20 resets throw both drivers (closing ≥ 31 m/s); derby 6 cars 0.36–1.47 per minute over three heats, 2 cars none in 3 × 90 s (no car is ever disabled); city race (4 AI, seed 1) 1 in 71 s.
- **Body** (`engine-ragdoll.ts` `RagdollSystem`): its own Rapier world (`rapier.ts` `loadRapier`, the game's one `@dimforge/rapier3d` 0.19.3: its own chunk plus a separate `.wasm`, 618 kB over the wire). It loads in the background once `CrashEngine.ready` resolves; boot never waits for it. A throw judged before it is in waits for it up to 1 s of sim, then is dropped.
  - 4 pooled 10-box dummies on spherical joints; a fifth throw recycles the oldest. Each lives 10 s. Drawn as one instanced mesh (`ragdoll-mesh.ts` `DummyMesh`): a rounded unit box scaled per part (capsule limbs, rounded head), instance colours for a dark tee, jeans, skin and short dark hair, plus render-only hands, shoes, hair, hips, shoulders and neck posed from their bodies. No texture, no new program.
  - **Driver looks** (`driver-look.ts` `driverLook(seed, slot)`): a pure function of the field's look seed and the car's slot, through its own `mulberry32`, never the sim's RNG (sim digests do not move). Tee from 16 colours picked to read against asphalt, sand and car paint; hair from 18 (natural black to blond and grey, ginger, auburn, and 2 dyed: blue, pink); 10% of drivers are women (`WOMAN_RATE`): narrower shoulders and chest, wider hips, hair at the sides and back of the head, falling over her shoulders, and a ponytail. A cop keeps the navy uniform; only the hair under the cap varies. The seed is `RaceField.look` (rolled with each new field and sent in `MSG.race`, so clients and the reel agree; a clip stores it: `REPLAY_VERSION` 5), `derbyRound` in a derby, the run's scene seed in the sandbox.
  - **Purse** (`ragdoll-purse.ts` `Purses`, civilian women only): on her throw a purse (a small box with a strap, one of 7 colours) leaves beside her on her heading at 0.85× her speed; at 0.15 s 4-7 of 7 small things (lipstick, phone, keys, two coins, a compact, a pen) burst out of it on its heading within atan(0.2) ≈ 11° at 0.85× its speed. Cosmetic Rapier bodies in the dummies' world (`G_PROPS` group: they hit the ground, the barrier, the walls and every car, not dummies or each other; their own car for the first 0.35 s excepted); one pooled set per dummy slot (8 bodies, drawn in one instanced mesh), cleared with the dummy, a reset and a dispose. Every roll is a `mulberry32` of the look seed and the car, so a replayed throw repeats.
  - Off a course, the ground (flat pad or disc) and the derby bowl wall are fixed colliders built once at a run's first throw and shared by every dummy; on a course, each throw gets a heightfield patch and the walls near it.
  - Every car (and the jersey barrier) is a kinematic box pair (bumper to bonnet line, then the cabin) that follows its pose: cars push dummies, and nothing pushes back.
  - **Fixed step, soft body** (lane ragdoll-6): the world steps a fixed 1/120 s out of an accumulator and the draw blends the last two steps; the kinematic cars move along each frame's path step by step. It used to step once per frame with that frame's dt, and Rapier warm-starts its contacts and joints with the last step's impulse, so a frame rate or a hitch changed the throw (range probe: landings 17.7–58.1 m across rates and jittered frames, a joint 43 cm apart, one step gaining 5.1 kJ; now 27.8–29.3 m at 30–360 Hz and jittered frames, joints under 2.2 cm, no frame gaining more than 3 J). No CCD on the parts (it tears the joints) and 8 solver iterations. The skin is a crumple zone (`ragdoll-body.ts`): a part that has just hit something (its velocity jumps 4 m/s in one step, counted once per hit) sheds half its motion across each of its joints, and no joint spins past 12 rad/s, both through equal and opposite impulses, so energy only leaves. From his first touch of fixed ground he is damped hard, and harder once his torso lies still; once his whole body is under 8 J for 0.5 s he sleeps (a car driving into him wakes and shoves him).
  - A dummy's own parts collide with each other except jointed neighbours, and dummies collide with each other. For its first 0.35 s it ignores its own car's cabin (it starts inside it), every car's lower box (a crushed pair's rest-size noses reach past the real ones) and the other dummies; every other car's cabin it hits from the first frame.
- **Glass**: the authority (host or offline) smashes the exit pane with `smashGlass` at the hit, and clients take it from the glass bits.
- **Cosmetic**: the cars never feel a dummy; sim digests are identical with ragdolls on and off (`ragdoll-trigger.test.ts`, and the sweep's fleet, derby and race runs).
- **Sandbox only** (neither a race nor a derby): the camera rides with every dummy still moving (a head-on's two together, pulled back to fit them) unless it follows another car or the user framed the shot, until they all lie still. The ride starts at the throw (the crash cam's cuts wait while a driver is out; its letterbox and clock run on, and the slow-mo onset is unchanged): it opens on the windshield (`present/ride-cam.ts`: a low eye 1.6 or 2 m up on the thrown car's forward axis, as far ahead as the high eye would stand (past where the dummy is when the slow-mo starts: nearer, he flew through the lens), when it passes `camUsable` (2 m of room round it, and sight of the head now and 0.25 s on), else a high one 3-6.5 m up and clear of every solid. A head-on's other car, the other cars of a pile-up and a barrier at the head's height block the low eye, so it stands only on open ground), eases into following the dummy (behind or ahead, alternating per ride), and every few seconds the trackside cinematic (`CineCam`, a fixed eye ahead of the dummy's travel) takes a turn (not in the range). Shot changes, cuts to another dummy and the return from the user's orbit ease from the camera's pose (0.9-3 s, longer for a longer fly; an eye over 90 m off is cut to), and the aim turns at most 4 rad/s. When the ride ends (all still; the moment is unchanged) the camera eases from its last pose into the engine's orbit over 1 s (smootherstep), not in one frame. A drag (mouse or touch, the orbit's own input) orbits the camera around the dummy, the orbit's pitch limits; the ride's shot changes wait during the drag and 2.5 s after it, then the rotation resumes from that view. The ride only takes the camera.
- **Range scene** (`scenes/range.ts`, art in `present/range-art.ts`, HUD Range button): FlatOut's driver-flinging event. Car A runs up 40 m at 100 km/h into the jersey barrier (hood height); the hit throws its driver over it into a sand field (the flat ground's friction 3 wins over the dummy's 0.12) with a numbered sign every 10 m to 100 m. The camera watches him from behind until he lies still, the HUD shows the distance (live, then landed), and with Loop on the next run starts 3 s later. The art is built on first entry and warmed then, never at boot.
- The jointed body follows Matthias von Bargen's [rapierjs-ragdoll](https://github.com/mattvb91/rapierjs-ragdoll) (MIT License; the notice is in the `engine-ragdoll.ts` header).

## Race tracks
`engine-marks.ts` exports `markMapUniforms`, `applyMarkMap(material)` and `setMarkBounds(minX, minZ, maxX, maxZ)`.
- A track's road, runoff and terrain materials call `applyMarkMap` once. It samples by world xz, so it works on hilly courses.
- `RaceDirector` calls `setMarkBounds(track.bounds…)` on load, and the sandbox rect (±48 m) on exit. Either call also clears the map.

## Bench
Run the A/B on the same build with `scripts/bench-browser.mjs --url "<dev url>/?fx=off"` against the default URL (high tier).

The first A/B (2026-10-01, RTX 4080 via d3d12) ran at load average 35–70 from another project. Even the `off` tier swung from 3.6 to 14.8 fps between two back-to-back runs, so those numbers can't resolve the +1.5 ms budget.

The draw-call delta is stable:
- post chain: +11 calls (scene RT, 5 + 4 bloom passes, composite) at high;
- streaks and tyre smoke: +1 each while alive;
- mark stamp: ≤ +1 per frame with slip.

Re-measure on a quiet box.
