# Cinematic FX

Visual and camera effects that read sim state only. Nothing here feeds back into the physics, so the
physics tests are unchanged. The only sim-side effect is the hit-stop, which scales the sim clock
(`timeWarp`) for 0.09 s on the driven car's big hits, the same way slow-mo already does.

**Tiers.** Choose them with HUD Playback → FX `off / minimal / low / high / ultra / Auto`, the `F` key (cycles), or `?fx=off|minimal|low|high|ultra` in the URL (for bench A/B). The default is Auto (Defaults restores it); the HUD shows the current tier and whether Auto is on.

**Ultra** (`present/ultra/`, manual only: `AutoFx` never lifts to it, and a pick turns Auto off). It runs the `high` post chain (the same composite) and adds a photographic look: an HDRI sky (`public/ultra/sky.hdr`) as the environment, turned so its sun lies where the sun light does; the sun's shadow on a 2048 map with a texel-derived normal bias and a wider soft edge (radius 3); and PBR sets (colour, normals at 2×, occlusion at 2×, roughness) over the asphalt, concrete and natural ground. It is a lazy chunk: the code (4 kB) and 3.4 MB of images load on the first pick (the button reads "loading…"; a failed fetch logs and leaves the tier). The first switch links ten more programs (the dressed surfaces) and uploads the textures: the longest frame in a live city race was 0.77–1.0 s (three fresh pages), the second switch no longer than a normal frame. Per frame it draws the same calls and triangles as `high`, and its GPU cost against `high` is not resolved from zero (24 paired rounds on an RTX 4080 laptop: −0.2 ± 0.5 ms). All of it is CC0 from Poly Haven; sources are in the header of `present/ultra/ultra.ts`.

**Auto** (`present/auto-fx.ts` `AutoFx`, applied per frame by `fxFrame`). One policy for the whole session, boot to race to reel:
- Load check: boot starts on `minimal`. On a fine-pointer device with a known hardware GPU (`hardwareDesktop`: not SwiftShader, llvmpipe, softpipe or the Basic Render Driver), once `ready` resolves it skips 1.5 s, then judges one 1 s window. Nothing waits on it.
- What it reads (`present/frame-work.ts` `FrameWork`): frame WORK against a 60 fps budget (16.7 ms), whatever the display's refresh, not the frame rate: under vsync the rate is pinned at the refresh whether a frame takes 3 ms or 16 ms. A window's figures are the median main-thread ms of a frame (`Engine.workMs`: sim plus draw submit; the previous frame's, since this one's draw is not yet done) and, where the browser has a GPU timer (`present/gpu-timer.ts`, `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2`), the median GPU ms (used when at least 10 % of the window's frames brought a reading); the busier of the two as a share of the budget is `busy`. Room to spend: `busy` under 0.75 (the top tier costs at most 0.2 of the budget more than `minimal`, measured). Too full: `busy` over 1. With no GPU reading (Firefox, some phones) the wall rate backs it up: under 50 fps is too full, and room also needs the median wall interval within 57 fps, so a GPU-bound frame only the timer would show still counts through the wall rate.
- Monitor: after every switch it skips 1.5 s (the first post-chain frames), then judges 1 s windows. Two windows in a row too full step down one tier: `high` → `low` → `minimal`, and the check starts again on the new tier. A single hitch, however long, lands in one window, so it never drops a tier. A tier that held climbs back one tier at a time after 8 windows in a row with room, never before 20 windows after a step down; a climb dropped again inside 20 windows doubles the streak the next climb needs (up to 64), so a load that comes and goes does not flap. `ultra` is the user's pick: Auto never climbs into it.
- Matches: a race (or a derby) starts on `minimal`, for headroom at the start: from the grid, through the countdown, until green + 3 s (the race clock, or the derby clock that runs from −4.5 s to green at 0). Then the load check runs once more: a 1 s window with room lifts the tier back to the ceiling (the highest tier that has held this session; `low` on a box that dropped to `low`); otherwise it stays `minimal` until two windows with room (a tier that held before) lift it. From the lift on the monitor runs as usual. When the race ends (results, the reel, back to setup) the tier returns to the ceiling, so a capable desktop watches the results reel on `high`.
- A manual pick (a tier button, `F`, or `?fx=`) turns Auto off for the session; the Auto button or Defaults turns it back on. Each switch logs `Crush Stream FX auto: <tier> (last window <n> fps, <n> % of the frame budget)` to the console.
- `off`: the renderer draws straight to the canvas. There's no post chain, no mark map, no crash cam, no hit punch, and no tyre smoke from slip. Sparks and glass use the plain look.
- `minimal`: the same straight-to-canvas render as `off` (no HDR target, bloom, grade, vignette, grain or blur; flash, chromatic punch and letterbox need the composite, so they don't show), plus every effect that needs no post: tyre marks (1024² map), tyre smoke and slip sparks, shake and FOV kick, hit-stop, crash-cam cuts. Sparks and glass use the plain look (no streaks, no HDR glow). A fixed-seed capture of `minimal` is identical to `off` (max channel difference 0).
- Every tier's programs and both outputs (canvas, HDR target) are linked and drawn once at boot and when a race course loads, so a tier switch or a race never links a program (`docs/PERF_HITCH.md`), except the first pick of Ultra (above).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, flash, chromatic punch, radial blur or crash-cam moves at any tier. The crash-cam cuts still happen but hold still.

Keys: `F` FX tier · `H` night · `X` wet asphalt (also HUD Playback → Night / Wet).

## Thrown drivers (ragdolls)
A driver flies out of a car on a disabling hit: through the windshield on a head-on, or through the struck side's front window on a side hit. Nothing is thrown on a rear hit, a scrape, or a hit that leaves the car sound. The dummy is cosmetic and local: no dummy state enters the sim or the snapshots.
- **Trigger** (`ragdoll-trigger.ts` `EjectionWatch`), once per car per run, on the edge where the car is disabled: its drivetrain dies (a derby kill or wreck, a race DNF by damage), or its engine block is packed back past the kill travel of the slider's realistic end (`killTravel(class, 1, ctx)`: the sourced 0.15 m × durability, × the derby scale in a derby). The second edge is a hit that would disable a real car: at the HUD's default realism (0.25) a fleet sedan only dies at 0.45 m of travel and one head-on tops out at 0.36 m, so no single sandbox or race hit ever kills (by design; the sim is untouched). The hit must be head-on or from a side (`impactInward`), and the peak closing speed over the 0.35 s before it must be at least 6 m/s. Everything it reads (drivetrain flag, `engineTravel`, impact, poses, velocities) is replicated, so a netplay client decides the same way as the host.
- **Rate at default settings** (lane ragdoll-4's headless sweep): fleet 2 cars at 0–32 m/s, 8 of 20 resets throw both drivers (closing ≥ 31 m/s); derby 6 cars 0.36–1.47 per minute over three heats, 2 cars none in 3 × 90 s (no car is ever disabled); city race (4 AI, seed 1) 1 in 71 s.
- **Body** (`engine-ragdoll.ts` `RagdollSystem`): its own Rapier world (`rapier.ts` `loadRapier`, the game's one `@dimforge/rapier3d-simd` 0.19.3, the SIMD wasm build of the same API: its own chunk plus a separate `.wasm`, 618 kB over the wire). It loads in the background once `CrashEngine.ready` resolves; boot never waits for it. A throw judged before it is in waits for it up to 1 s of sim, then is dropped.
  - 4 pooled 10-box dummies on spherical joints; a fifth throw recycles the oldest. Each lives 10 s. Drawn as one instanced mesh (`ragdoll-mesh.ts` `DummyMesh`): a rounded unit box scaled per part (capsule limbs, rounded head), instance colours for a dark tee, jeans, skin and short dark hair, plus render-only hands, shoes, hair, hips, shoulders and neck posed from their bodies. No texture, no new program.
  - **Driver looks** (`driver-look.ts` `driverLook(seed, slot)`): a pure function of the field's look seed and the car's slot, through its own `mulberry32`, never the sim's RNG (sim digests do not move). Tee from 16 colours picked to read against asphalt, sand and car paint; hair from 18 (natural black to blond and grey, ginger, auburn, and 2 dyed: blue, pink); 10% of drivers are women (`WOMAN_RATE`): narrower shoulders and chest, wider hips, hair at the sides and back of the head, falling over her shoulders, and a ponytail. A cop keeps the navy uniform; only the hair under the cap varies. The seed is `RaceField.look` (rolled with each new field and sent in `MSG.race`, so clients and the reel agree; a clip stores it: `REPLAY_VERSION` 5), `derbyRound` in a derby, the run's scene seed in the sandbox.
  - **Purse** (`ragdoll-purse.ts` `Purses`, civilian women only): on her throw a purse (a small box with a strap, one of 7 colours) leaves beside her on her heading at 0.85× her speed; at 0.15 s 4-7 of 7 small things (lipstick, phone, keys, two coins, a compact, a pen) burst out of it on its heading within atan(0.2) ≈ 11° at 0.85× its speed. Cosmetic Rapier bodies in the dummies' world (`G_PROPS` group: they hit the ground, the barrier, the walls and every car, not dummies or each other; their own car for the first 0.35 s excepted); one pooled set per dummy slot (8 bodies, drawn in one instanced mesh), cleared with the dummy, a reset and a dispose. Every roll is a `mulberry32` of the look seed and the car, so a replayed throw repeats.
  - **Knocked props** (`ragdoll-props.ts` `PropBodies`; a course's and the Lab's cones, crates, tyre stacks and hay bales): each standing one is a fixed body on its spot in the footprint a car meets (so a dummy meets it as a car does); when a car knocks it (`propContact`, the race's own rule: the sim's knock and its count never read this world) or the Lab flicks it, it turns dynamic in its own shape, flies off as the knock sends it (below), lands and comes to rest, drawn where it lies (`PropTumble`). It hits the ground, walls, dummies, every car (the one that knocked it after 0.35 s) and the other knocked props; it shares a ground patch with every knocked prop it covers and moves to a new one past 8 m inside its edge. A knock before Rapier is in flies once it is; the standing bodies go when their course's ground does, and a reset stands every prop again.
    - **The knock** (lane props-rate): a car's bumper (the band of its proxy's lower box a standing prop meets, 0.05-0.85 m up) meets a standing prop at the middle of the part of its height that band spans and drives that point out at the knock's plan speed; the push that does it, at that point's height over the centre of mass, sets the spin through the prop's own inertia, and the centre of mass keeps the rest of the speed. That spin swings an edge of its base down into the road, which pushes up on that edge: the two pushes are solved together, so the edge goes down no faster than the knock lifts the prop, and the prop rises no higher than the knock's lift throws it (the bumper's push alone spun a cone so fast that the road vaulted it: 18.8 m up from a car at 42 m/s against the lift's own 8.1; now 7.8). Knocked at 11 m/s: a cone (met 0.37 m up, centre of mass 0.16 m) leaves at 6.1 m/s spinning 22.5 rad/s and tips over; a crate or tyre stack (met 0.05 m under theirs) turns back 3-5 rad/s and tips; a hay bale (met at its own) slides on upright. A Lab flick (no car) sets no spin. The course's heightfield has its inner edges fixed (`FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES`) and its vertices on the world's 2 m lattice: before, props tipped only by catching seams (a crate slid flat at 11 m/s tipped 88°; it now slides 7 m upright, as its friction says).
    - **Step and ground** (lane props-rate): with no dummy out and no knocked prop moving fast the props' world steps 1/120 s; it steps 1/480 while a dummy is out or any point of a knocked prop's own shape moves over 8 m/s (its middle's speed plus its turn times its reach: at 1/120 a spinning cone flung at 31 m/s landed with its tip 110 mm in the ground). A knocked prop has CCD and 0.1 m soft CCD, so a fast one meets a wall it would cross in a step (48 knocks at a road wall at 30-70 m/s: none through it; 9 without CCD). Its ground patch is 32 m across, centred 7 m down the knock (a dummy's stays 96 m and 24 m). Headless City, 32 cars, seeds 2-4 (main f90e3604 -> lane): the props' world takes 0.17-0.35 -> 0.14-0.29 ms a frame and 0.49-0.62 -> 0.29-0.53 ms a stepping frame.
  - Off a course, the ground (flat pad or disc) and the derby bowl wall are fixed colliders built once at a run's first throw and shared by every dummy; on a course, each throw gets a heightfield patch and the walls near it.
  - Every car within 10 m of a dummy or a purse thing (and the jersey barrier), or able to touch a knocked prop within a frame (closer than both their reaches plus both their travels that frame), is a kinematic set of cuboids fitted to its drawn body that follows its body's pose (`ragdoll-proxy.ts` `ProxyFitter`, refitted when `car.cage.serial` moves: columns from 1 m under the ground to the cage's top over the hood, the deck and every other part under the belt (1 m), a slab at the roof where the cage is over it, the lower box under the cabin to the sill (0.8 m) and a slab at each pane of its glass, a crushed roof taking its glass down; at most 40 a car, the cage's top held within 3 cm of each column and slab, measured by `ragdoll-proxy-fit.test.ts`): cars push dummies and props, and nothing pushes back. Before each frame's steps (`PropBodies.press`), a knocked prop within its reach of a car's lower box and of a static box near it (a wall or a box solid; the ground is a heightfield), the ways out of the two boxes to its middle opposing (cosine under -0.1), lets that car by until the box no longer covers it: the box would drive it into the static (a car scraping a wall over a cone lying at its foot left it 0.2 m inside the wall; in 75 such scrapes the cone now gets at most 9.3 mm in, 118 before). It is read from the boxes' geometry in typed-array scratch, not Rapier's contacts, whose per-pair callbacks allocated 3.4-5.5 KB a frame in City; run-overs on open ground are unchanged. The lower box reaching under the ground leaves no gap under a car for a lying body to wedge into: with its bottom 5 cm up, a car driving into a lying cone pressed it into the road (31 of 80 run-overs at 10-40 m/s over 32 mm, one left 0.66 m under it); now none over 23 mm. A road wall's box reaches 1 m under the ground for the same reason (a cone slid into its foot went 46 mm in). A knocked prop at rest (under 0.1 m/s and 0.1 rad/s) for 0.25 s goes to sleep (Rapier alone waits 2 s), and a sleeping one is not read.
  - **Fixed step, soft body** (lane ragdoll-6): the world steps a fixed step (1/120 s then, 1/960 s now: see the next bullet) out of an accumulator and the draw blends the last two steps; the kinematic cars move along each frame's path step by step. It used to step once per frame with that frame's dt, and Rapier warm-starts its contacts and joints with the last step's impulse, so a frame rate or a hitch changed the throw (range probe: landings 17.7–58.1 m across rates and jittered frames, a joint 43 cm apart, one step gaining 5.1 kJ; now 27.8–29.3 m at 30–360 Hz and jittered frames, joints under 2.2 cm, no frame gaining more than 3 J). No CCD on the parts (it tears the joints) and 8 solver iterations at 1/120 (4 at 1/960). The skin is a crumple zone (`ragdoll-body.ts`): a part that has just hit something (its velocity jumps 4 m/s in 1/120 s, counted once per hit) sheds half its motion across each of its joints, and no joint spins past 12 rad/s, both through equal and opposite impulses, so energy only leaves. From his first touch of fixed ground he is damped hard, and harder once his torso lies still; once his whole body is under 8 J for 0.5 s he sleeps (a car driving into him wakes and shoves him).
  - **Step rate** (lane ragdoll-rate; owner 2026-10-04: "the ragdoll seems significantly lower fps than the vehicles, especially in slo-mo"): the step is a constant 1/960 s (it was 1/120), 4 solver iterations x 2 internal passes (was 8 x 2), at most 0.05 sim s of steps a frame (was 0.1, as the cars). Cause, measured: not a stale blend (0 plateaus; the drawn head moved `speed x dt` every frame, ratio 1.00 at p5-p95, at 60 and 240 Hz) but a coarse step: slow-mo is 0.032x and the reel runs the same curve (`clipTimeline`), so a 1/120 step is 273 ms of wall time, 3.7 new poses a real second, drawn as straight chords with a velocity kink at every step and 1 step (8.3 sim ms, 0.23 m at 28 m/s) behind the cars. A step per drawn frame at 240 Hz and 0.032x is 1/7500 s (125 steps a frame at 1x and 60 Hz, ~11 ms with four dummies out), more than the cost holds; 1/960 is 30.7 poses a real second (33 ms apart) and 1.04 sim ms behind the cars. A constant rate, so a throw is the same live, in slow-mo and in a reel replay.
    - The hit rule (`HIT_DV` 4 m/s), the 12 rad/s spin limit and the purses are still judged every 1/120 s (`WATCH`, every 8th step): per step at 1/960 the hit rule gave 82-91 skin gives a range throw against 21-23 (a hit runs more steps; its gaps re-armed it), and the spin limit per step measured no different (max pre-clamp joint spin 43-90 rad/s both ways). Two iterations kicked a joint to 125 rad/s at the first touch; one internal pass gained 26-30 J in 1 of 5 runs at 1/480 and at 1/960.
    - Nothing is stepped while every dummy and purse thing lies asleep and no car moving over 0.5 m/s or barrier is within 10 m of a dummy (`dormant`; a wreck lying beside him does not wake the world; the proxies jump to the cars on the first step after). It cut the 60 Hz range throw from 16 steps a frame to 0 for 77 % of its real-speed frames.

## Race tracks
`engine-marks.ts` exports `markMapUniforms`, `applyMarkMap(material)` and `setMarkBounds(minX, minZ, maxX, maxZ)`.
- A track's road, runoff and terrain materials call `applyMarkMap` once. It samples by world xz, so it works on hilly courses.
- `RaceDirector` calls `setMarkBounds(track.bounds…)` on load, and the sandbox rect (±48 m) on exit. Either call also clears the map.

## Bench
Run the A/B on the same build with `scripts/bench-browser.mjs --url "<dev url>/?fx=off"` against the default URL (high tier).

The first A/B (2026-10-01, RTX 4080 via d3d12) ran at load average 35–70 from another project. Even the `off` tier swung from 3.6 to 14.8 fps between two back-to-back runs, so those numbers can't resolve the +1.5 ms budget.

The draw-call delta is stable:
- post chain: +11 calls (scene RT, 5 + 4 bloom passes, composite) at high;
- streaks and tyre smoke: +1 each while alive;
- mark stamp: ≤ +1 per frame with slip.

Re-measure on a quiet box.
