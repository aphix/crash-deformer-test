The José Martí Memorial is a 109 m tower in the form of a five-pointed star, set on a star-shaped base with a statue of Martí in front; the full monument is often described as rising to about 142 m above sea level when the hill and base are included.[1][2][4][10]

It sits on Loma de los Catalanes, a small hill that was used to raise the plaza above the surrounding city, and the memorial itself is described as being about 30 m above sea level on that hill.[4][11] The plaza around it is a very large paved civic square of about 72,000 m², roughly 270 m by 270 m if treated as a square, though the real footprint is more open and irregular.[1][8][11]

## Real layout
- The memorial occupies the north side of Plaza de la Revolución.[1][11]
- The plaza is framed by grassy, terraced or ramped slopes around the monument area rather than a fully flat open field.[11][14]
- The surrounding urban edges include the Ministry of the Interior on the north with the Che Guevara façade, the Ministry of Communications on the south with the Camilo Cienfuegos façade, the Palace of the Revolution behind the memorial, the National Library on the east, and the National Theatre on the west.[1][11]
