# Query

How do FlatOut 2 and FlatOut Ultimate Carnage stage the driver windshield ejection (ragdoll) in crashes: where exactly does the driver ragdoll spawn relative to the car (inside cabin, above hood, at the windshield), how is the slow-motion timed relative to the impact (does it start immediately or shortly after), and what visual effects (glass shards, debris, dust, camera cut) hide the moment the driver exits the car? Include any developer commentary (Bugbear) or modding/reverse-engineering notes about the ejection spawn point and timing.

# Result

FlatOut 2 and FlatOut: Ultimate Carnage both stage the driver ejection as a windshield-launch event, with the ragdoll effectively spawning at the front of the car and then being thrown forward through the glass rather than appearing far away from the vehicle[1][3][11]. Public descriptions of the series consistently describe the driver being “tossed through the windshield” or ejected from the cabin during high-impact crashes, and in the racing modes the effect is usually presented as a dramatic crash moment rather than a freely visible transition[1][3][6][11].

The slow-motion in these games is generally tied to the crash highlight itself, so it begins right as the ejection sequence is triggered or extremely shortly after the impact, not after a long delay[6][11]. That timing helps obscure the exact instant the driver leaves the car, because the camera shifts into a cinematic crash view almost immediately.

The visual masking comes from a combination of glass-shattering, debris, dust, motion blur, and the camera cut into the crash-ragdoll presentation[6][11]. In practical terms, the windshield break and surrounding impact effects cover the precise spawn/extraction point, so the driver’s exit looks like a single continuous burst through the windshield instead of a clearly visible pop-out.

For modding and reverse-engineering notes, the available public commentary is mostly indirect: community references describe the driver as being ejected “through the windshield,” and mod-oriented notes indicate that FlatOut 2 and Ultimate Carnage still support ragdoll/ejection behavior through mods and tools, but do not document a clearly separate visible spawn point somewhere in open space[11][13]. In other words, the commonly reported behavior is that the ragdoll is created at or just behind the windshield area, then launched forward under the crash camera and slow-motion presentation[1][11][13].
